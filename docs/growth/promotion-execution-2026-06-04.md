## 3. Reddit And Communities

The generated Reddit dry-run is too promotional for cold community posting. Use education-first versions manually.

Education post title:

```text
Checklist I use before emailing a Korean cosmetics manufacturer
```

Body:

```text
I have been looking at Korean supplier research workflows and the biggest mistake I see is emailing the first marketplace listing without checking basic signals.

For cosmetics or skincare OEM/ODM, I would check:

1. Does the company explicitly mention OEM or ODM?
2. Is there a real company website, not only a marketplace page?
3. Are MFDS, GMP, ISO, export, or brand authorization signals mentioned?
4. Does the supplier show MOQ, lead time, sample, or packaging information?
5. Can you write a specific RFQ instead of "please send catalog"?

A better first RFQ includes product type, target quantity, destination country, packaging needs, required certificates, sample request, and target timeline.

Disclosure: I am building a tool called GloSource AI that helps generate supplier candidate shortlists and RFQ drafts for Korea sourcing. I am sharing the checklist because it is useful even if you do the research manually.
```
