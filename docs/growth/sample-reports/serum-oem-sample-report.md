## 2. Manufacturer vs trader signal

- ✅ **Manufacturer signals:** own factory address + CGMP/MFDS facility registration; R&D/formulation pages; brand client list; "ODM/OEM" with in-house lab.
- ⚠️ **Trader/reseller signals:** marketplace-only presence; no factory address; "we can source anything"; reluctance to share factory/cert docs; pricing without spec.
- For each candidate: ask "Are you the manufacturer of record? What is your MFDS facility number?" A real factory answers fast.

## 3. Certification signal (verify the actual certificate, not the claim)

- **MFDS** (Korea cosmetics regulator) facility registration — baseline.
- **CGMP / ISO 22716** (cosmetics GMP) — quality system.
- **ISO 9001** — general quality mgmt.
- **Vegan / cruelty-free** (e.g., relevant certs) if your brand needs it.
- **US: FDA + MoCRA** facility registration if importing to the US (required since MoCRA).
- Ask for **certificate copies + numbers**; cross-check expiry and scope.

## 4. MOQ estimate (confirm — varies by formula/packaging)

- Established majors: often **3,000–10,000+ units/SKU** (ODM custom formula).
- Indie/low-MOQ factories: sometimes **500–1,000 units** (stock formula / private label).
- Custom (OEM) is higher than stock-formula (ODM/private label). Ask MOQ **per SKU + per packaging type** separately.

## 5. Risk note

- "Low MOQ" claims may exclude packaging/printing minimums — ask total landed minimum.
- Trader markup + spec drift if not dealing with the real factory.
- Lead times 6–8+ weeks; sampling adds weeks. Plan launch accordingly.
- MoCRA/FDA + EU CPSR compliance is **your** responsibility as importer — confirm the factory can supply documentation.
- Always order **paid samples + a small first run** before scaling.

## 8. Buyer checklist (before you commit)

- [ ] Certificate copies received + numbers verified (MFDS, CGMP/ISO 22716)
- [ ] MOQ confirmed **per SKU and per packaging**, total landed minimum
- [ ] Unit price at 2 quantity tiers + sample cost
- [ ] Lead time + sampling time in writing
