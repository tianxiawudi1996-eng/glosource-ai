## 2026 Korean cosmetics law: primary packaging vs secondary packaging
Under Cosmetics Act Enforcement Decree Art. 2 effective 2026-04-02, cosmetics MANUFACTURING business includes first-stage/primary packaging of cosmetics, but this does not mean generic printed boxes and secondary-only packaging require cosmetics manufacturing registration. Responsible selling includes direct manufacture-and-sale, consignment-made cosmetics distribution, and imported cosmetics distribution. Source: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lumLsLinkPop.do?chrClsCd=010202&lspttninfSeq=150976 . Accordingly, exhibitor 092 SIP is **PACKAGING_PRIMARY_VS_SECONDARY_UNDETERMINED**, not conclusively MFDS-out-of-scope. Other apparent brand/device/service entities also require case-by-case business-role inquiry before exclusion.
