## Matching method
1. Capture complete official KINTEX exhibitor roster and booth number; do not use Osong roster.
2. Classify Korean cosmetics manufacturer, Korean responsible cosmetics seller, packaging, equipment, retailer, overseas entity and unknown.
3. Compare qualified Korean companies using legally verified identity or business number, then exact Korean legal name plus address. Similar English names do not prove a match.
4. Query real MFDS data and GloSource AI kr_cosmetics_firms table; record last refresh time.
5. Report confirmed, candidate, unresolved, excluded and not found separately. No success rate before proper denominator and DB access.
