# Global K-Product Sourcing Contract

## Job To Be Done

Help international buyers discover Korean manufacturer and supplier candidates, review public registry evidence, and prepare an English RFQ.

## Commercial Contract

- Entry route: `/en`
- Commercial status: live; buyers can purchase now through Polar MoR
- Language and currency: English / USD
- Checkout: Polar
- Anonymous search: disabled
- Verified free member: 3 searches per month
- Free result: one supplier preview; full details and exports remain locked
- Starter: 39 USD, 40 searches per month
- Pro: 99 USD, 100 searches per month
- Business: 249 USD, 300 searches per month

## Trust Contract

- “Registry Match” means the candidate identity matched an applicable public Korean registry record.
- Registry evidence, AI estimates, and live business-status evidence must remain visibly distinct.
- GloSource does not audit, certify, endorse, or guarantee a supplier.
- Buyers must confirm identity, capacity, commercial terms, samples, and compliance before ordering.

## Access Rules

- Authentication and email verification are required for free searches.
- Global usage is logged with `surface = 'en'`.
- Toss subscriptions must never increase global quotas.
- The dashboard, account, and billing pages show only Polar subscriptions.

## Primary Metrics

- `/en` visitor-to-registration conversion
- Verified-account-to-first-search activation
- Preview-to-Polar conversion
- RFQ/export usage
- 30-day paid retention and USD MRR
