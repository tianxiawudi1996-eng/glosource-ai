## 2. Manufacturer vs trader signal
Same rules as serum + **sunscreen-specific:** a real sun manufacturer can provide **in-vivo/in-vitro SPF & PA test reports** and discuss the regulatory path. Traders cannot.

## 3. Certification signal (sunscreen = functional → stricter)
- **MFDS functional cosmetic** approval (Korea treats sunscreen as functional) — critical.
- **SPF / PA test reports** (in-vivo SPF, in-vitro/UVA) from accredited labs.
- **CGMP/ISO 22716**, ISO 9001.
- **US:** sunscreen is an **OTC drug under FDA** — confirm the factory understands US OTC monograph + facility registration; this is a major risk if missed.
- **EU:** CPSR + UV filter compliance.

## 4. MOQ estimate (confirm)
- Functional formulas often carry **higher MOQ + testing cost** than basic skincare; majors 5,000–10,000+; some indie factories 1,000–3,000. Testing/registration fees are extra.

## 5. Risk note
- **US OTC drug classification** for sunscreen is the #1 trap — non-compliant imports get blocked. Confirm the factory's US-export experience.
- SPF claim substantiation must match label — demand test reports.
- Texture/whitecast differs by market; sample across skin tones.
- Longer lead + testing time; budget for it.
