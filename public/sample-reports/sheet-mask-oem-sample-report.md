## 2. Manufacturer vs trader signal
Same rules. Mask-specific: a real factory discusses **sheet material (cotton/microfiber/bio-cellulose/hydrogel)**, essence fill volume, and pouch printing in-house. Traders deflect material/spec questions.

## 3. Certification signal
- **MFDS** facility registration.
- **CGMP / ISO 22716**, ISO 9001.
- **Vegan / cruelty-free / dermatologically tested** if claimed — get the proof.
- **US: FDA + MoCRA** facility registration for US import.
- **EU:** CPSR.

## 4. MOQ estimate (confirm)
- Often the **lowest-MOQ** K-beauty category: stock essence + your pouch from **1,000–3,000** units common; fully custom essence higher. Pouch printing has its own minimum — ask separately.

## 5. Risk note
- "Low MOQ" sometimes means stock essence only; custom essence raises MOQ — clarify.
- Pouch print minimums + plate fees can exceed the mask minimum — ask total.
- Essence leakage / sheet fit / material quality vary — sample before scaling.
- MoCRA/CPSR compliance is the importer's responsibility.

## 8. Buyer checklist
- [ ] Manufacturer confirmed (MFDS facility no.), not trader
- [ ] Certs verified (MFDS, CGMP/ISO 22716, vegan if claimed)
- [ ] MOQ confirmed: **mask minimum + pouch-print minimum** separately
- [ ] Sheet material + essence fill confirmed in writing
- [ ] Unit price at 2 tiers + sample cost
- [ ] Lead + sampling time documented
- [ ] MoCRA (US) / CPSR (EU) docs available
- [ ] Paid sample evaluated (fit, essence, leakage) before PO
