## Product Signals

Foreign buyers do not search only for generic "skin lotion." They search around trend claims and retailer signals:

- Toner pads: calming, PDRN, collagen, tea tree, vitamin C, hyaluronic acid
- Barrier creams: ceramide, cica, microbiome, sensitive skin
- Serums and ampoules: PDRN, peptide, heartleaf, hyaluronic acid, collagen
- Masks: sheet masks, hydrogel masks, collagen masks
- Sunscreen: SPF50+ / PA++++, but high regulatory risk in US, EU, Brazil, and Mexico
- Olive Young-linked brands or styles: MEDIHEAL, Round Lab, Dr.G, d'Alba, Torriden, CLIO, Anua, Beauty of Joseon, VT, Biodance, rom&nd, Dasique

CJ Newsroom reported Olive Young's growth as a K-beauty signal and highlighted brand scale-up dynamics. Olive Young also established CJ Olive Young USA in Los Angeles in 2025, which makes US buyers more likely to search for Olive Young-adjacent product opportunities.

## Best RFQ Template For Foreign Buyers

Subject: RFQ - Korean K-Beauty Factory-Direct Skincare / Olive Young-Style Products

Hello,

We are a foreign beauty importer evaluating Korean skincare products for ecommerce, retail, and wholesale distribution.

Target products:

- Hydrating toner / essence toner
- Lightweight lotion / emulsion
- Barrier repair cream
- Toner pads
- Sheet masks / hydrogel masks
- Serums / ampoules
- Sunscreen SPF50+ / PA++++ if documentation supports our market

Please provide:

- MOQ per SKU and price breaks at 1,000 / 3,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 units
- FOB Korea and EXW pricing
- Sample cost and lead time
- Bulk production lead time
- Whether you are the actual manufacturer, sourcing agent, brand owner, or distributor
- Full INCI list, claims, shelf life, batch size, carton dimensions
- COA, SDS/MSDS, CFS, ISO 22716/GMP, stability, microbial, challenge test
- SPF/UVA reports if sunscreen
- Country-of-origin documents and export references
- Brand authorization letter if branded wholesale
- Label support for our target market
- Payment terms, refund policy, and sample-credit policy

We prefer suppliers with transparent manufacturer identity, stable repeat supply, and documentation that supports legal import into our market.

## Country-Specific Buyer Concerns

### United States

- Avoid ordinary cosmetics making drug-like claims.
- FDA does not "approve" normal cosmetics.
- MoCRA facility registration/product listing support matters.
- Sunscreen is high risk because it may be OTC drug territory.
- Amazon/FBA buyer asks for carton dimensions, batch/expiry coding, English label review, COA/SDS, and country of origin.

### UK/EU

- Needs Responsible Person, Product Information File, CPSR, label review, and CPNP/SCPN market notification.
- Sunscreen and actives require stronger evidence.
- Branded wholesale still needs market compliance, not only authenticity.

### Brazil

- ANVISA/import control is central.
- A Brazilian legal importer/responsible party is needed.
- Sunscreen is high risk and may require registration.
- Portuguese labeling, INCI, CFS, COA/SDS, stability, microbiology, challenge tests and SPF/UVA documents matter.

### Mexico

- COFEPRIS and NOM-141 labeling are the main buyer concerns.
- Spanish labeling, ingredient compliance, certificate of free sale, COO, COA/MSDS and import classification matter.
- Buyer may ask for under-invoicing or domestic Korean packaging only; reject this.

## Abuse Behaviors Observed In The Simulation

### Free-search abuse

- Repeats slight variants: toner, lotion, cream, serum, toner pad, sunscreen, mask
- Changes persona: Amazon FBA seller, UK importer, Brazil distributor, Mexico wholesaler
- Rotates countries to get different supplier lists
- Uses free searches to harvest company names, URLs, contact channels, and document requirements
- Copies RFQ outputs and contacts suppliers off-platform
- Searches branded wholesale and OEM separately to reconstruct GloSource's supplier graph

### Payment / refund abuse

- Subscribes cheaply, extracts supplier lists and RFQs, then cancels/refunds
- Claims the result was "not compliant for my country" after receiving usable leads
- Uses different email/company identities for repeated trials
- Requests free samples, free documents, and distributor pricing without proof of business
- Demands exclusivity for Brazil/Mexico without deposit or purchase commitment
- Uses regulatory excuses to demand free reformulation or relabeling work

1. Show partial supplier cards to free users; gate direct contact/export-ready details behind verified signup or paid plan.
