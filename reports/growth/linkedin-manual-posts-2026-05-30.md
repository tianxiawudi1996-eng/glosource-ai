## Post 1 - Publish Today

If you are sourcing Korean beauty or food products, the first supplier list is rarely the final answer.

The real job is filtering.

Before sending an RFQ, check:

1. Does the supplier look like a manufacturer, not only a trader?
2. Is there any export signal on EC21, BuyKorea/KOTRA, tradeKorea, or the company website?
3. Are certification mentions relevant to your category?
   - MFDS or GMP for cosmetics
   - HACCP or MFDS for food
   - KC for electronics
4. Can you write a specific RFQ with product, MOQ, packaging, destination, and sample request?

## Post 2 - Save For Next

Most sourcing mistakes happen before the first supplier reply.

The buyer sends:

"Hello, I want your product. What is the price?"

That is too vague.

A better RFQ to a Korean manufacturer includes:

- product category
- target MOQ
- destination country
- packaging requirements
- certificate needs
- sample request
- target timeline
